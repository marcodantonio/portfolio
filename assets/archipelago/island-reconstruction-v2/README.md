# Projects bare-island reconstruction V2

The human selected **island without buildings, followed by separate elements**.
The preceding `island-reconstruction-v1` native result is rejected and retained
outside gameplay; this is a fresh morphology, not its material-only refinement.

## Image first

`projects-turnaround-v2.png` was generated through Higgsfield from original
AM-4 (workshop) and AM-1 (whole map), with one completed job and no duplicate.
The actual output is 2880×2880, despite the requested 4k preset. Exact prompt,
media IDs, job, SHA and measured billing are in `provenance.json`.

It is a usable directional morphology guide: honey-earth apron, connected rear
terraces, front-left buttress/cleft, articulated front spurs and a lower right
headland. Its TOP is oblique, FRONT slightly down-tilted, panel scales are not
exact and a water strip was added unexpectedly. It is not orthographic CAD,
metric truth, or proof that a future mesh matches the original ten images.
