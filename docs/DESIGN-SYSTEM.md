# Design System

## Active direction

The [inventor archipelago](./design-directions/2026-09-27-inventor-archipelago/README.md)
is one tropical world with a dominant software/IT/AI identity and two smaller
personal destinations, Tesla and dance. The user's target is approximately
75% IT hierarchy, not three equally weighted home-page choices. That ratio is
a design target, not a measured content or pixel statistic.

Nine places are distributed across six physical territories. Shared land,
elongated ridges, varied elevations and one mechanical floating laboratory
replace the repeated equal-island pattern. Textual topology takes precedence
over inconsistent connections in concept images.

## Material and composition contract

Warm timber, terracotta roofs, grey-brown cliffs, sand, green vegetation, blue/
turquoise sea and restrained violet/amber identity accents form one palette.
Daylight is bright and slightly warm; existing stored night preference remains.
Tesla does not become a cyberpunk scene, and dance does not become a nightclub.

A place is identified primarily by architecture and objects, not a new colour
applied to an identical hut. Keep approaches clear, greenery concentrated near
edges, and one dominant silhouette per area. Do not spread the same waterfalls,
lanterns, flags or crates uniformly across every surface.

## Geography and rendering

Dance now uses a reference-led static22×16m Blender pavilion: open timber
pergola, cream curtains, lanterns, violet benches and attached flowers/vines,
with unobstructed central parquet and the two existing consultation objects.
Revision4's real153,778-triangle GLB is not reference-identical art approval.
The seven support decks share physics/render heights;71 supplemental stone/timber
foundation boxes use exact terrain-triangle footprint clipping, with24cm buried
feet. They add no walking plane. The middle Dance post requires a local approach
detour; the four bridge spans, endpoints,21 stations and196 props remain intact.
Current visual checks and remaining WebGL/material gaps belong to design-qa.md.

Contact adds the seventh structural deck on the same six physical territories,
not another island. Its tower foot root (17,.26,69) is distinct from the small
6.6×4.6×.24 m hut floor at (11.8,.26,71.7). Eighteen shared building hulls
include four local masonry toes, separate from the existing 71 clipped foundation
boxes. The Welcome-to-Dance path goes north of the hut via (15,64) → (23,65)
before the unchanged bridge shore; all six current terrain XYZ buffers, 196 props,
21 stations and bridge/arrival/transfer endpoints are retained in this batch.
This is a current freeze, not a ban on later user-authorized island reshaping:
future changes must coordinate terrain, hulls, bridges, map and safe approaches.

**Current elevation contract (1 October, later user correction):** normal land
must have low sandy shoulders, not high vertical plateaus. Welcome/About/Contact
sit 2.8/4.4/3.4 m above sea; Projects 4.8 m, Experience/Education 5.5/6.3 m,
Tesla 2.7 m and Dance 2.6 m. Their shared top meshes slope to a 0.38–0.72 m
sea-relative rim. This explicitly supersedes previous exact normal-top/rim-Y
and bridge-Y preservation claims in historical asset/coast work. Preserve XZ,
indices, routes and asset-local geometry; Skills alone retains its complete
old suspended mesh. Asset authoring Y is historical; runtime deck/root/hulls
are translated coherently by the shared map. Do not raise the ocean to fake it.

AREAS is semantic configuration; TERRITORIES is physical geometry. Shared terrain
vertices drive Three.js and Rapier. Bridges and boat ramps use the same deck
profiles for art and support. Changes to footprints/doors/props require collision
and safe-approach review. Decorative mesh detail does not justify invisible
barriers across a valid route.

Five territories—Main, Projects, Ridge, Tesla and Dance—must read as grounded
land meeting the sea, not flat discs on hanging piles of boulders. Low coastal
bedrock continues 10 m below WATER_LEVEL. Skills alone retains its original suspended underside and
mechanical apparatus; it does not receive sea-level shoreline foam. Terrain
paths are painted into a filtered material mask rather than layered as clipping
geometry.

The coastlines have actual sea channels, not merely recoloured seams. The
historical 1 October grounded-coast revision replaced the normal islands' rounded leaning
masses with vertical bedrock ribs and restrained shallow fracture faces. An
art-only profile shared by terrain and ocean derives foam/shallows from the real
rock/sea foot rather than the upper turf outline. Decorative plates stop above
the waterline contact band, and wetness is per fragment instead of tinting a
whole cell. Skills' previous tapered underside is unchanged. The warm grey-brown
palette and local mineral shoulders interrupt the uniform turf collar. These
decorative changes originally kept six exact top/rim buffers. The subsequent
natural-height correction genuinely lowers five shared normal terrain surfaces;
only their XZ/indices and Skills' full suspended surface remain exact. The low
shore's emerged toe is sand-colored and has no hanging cliff foliage. Bridge
approach ramps belong to the real shared mesh, protecting the flat deck aprons;
there is no new decorative or invisible walking ledge. Sand,
turf and timber have restrained antialiased variation; shadows, an owned sky
and filtered daylight/night environments provide coherent scene lighting.
Clouds remain soft procedurally lit atmospheric billboards, not volumetric fog.
Closed broad-leaf banana/monstera pockets, hibiscus and trailing stems add layered
botanical borders, while distant limestone clusters frame the horizon beyond
the navigable map. Full decorative footprints avoid building decks, routes,
stations and arrival/camera corridors; soft vegetation creates no new hulls.
Palms have solid pinnate leaflets, staggered young/older fronds and continuous
sand-brown bark with filtered detail instead of broad serrated blades and orange
segment blocks. The original trunk collision and conservative crown bounds
remain. Subtle owned wind is disabled by reduced motion; no alpha-card leaves
or new image/GLB generation is implied.
The latest botanical pass uses fuller bowed palm laminae and several soil-rooted
shoots per border pocket, with upper fans, middle broad leaves and low fringes,
instead of concentric rosettes. The existing full-radius approach guards remain.
Mineral aprons now resolve shallow irregular joints while paths remain earth and
coasts sand; this is filtered surface finish, not new raised tiles. Lower daytime
hemisphere/fill strengthens the existing warm-key/cool-shadow separation; the
night light contract is unchanged. Coastal foam has real gaps and staggered wash
fronts, retaining the same sea-contact field and one reflection owner. These are
procedural art refinements, not new paid assets, image-identical reconstruction
or measured performance approval. Current visual evidence belongs in design-qa.md.
The garage car has a continuous sculpted body/glass silhouette. Open portions
of the studio and garage roofs expose interiors; entrance sight corridors and
the stable three-quarter camera keep tall palms out of the initial view.
The walking lens now includes a sky band instead of looking down on an empty
floor; tall places have their own headroom, not a globally raised eye. Overview
is independently fitted and unchanged. Dance alone has finished clearcoated
parquet and two real warm lantern fills; weathered bridges, posts and furniture
remain matte. This is physical material response, not mirror-floor scenery or
reference images painted behind the player.

The opening copy is a compact card, not a large opaque veil over the world.
These changes improve legibility and modelling, but do not establish cinematic
reference fidelity, a production asset pipeline or final visual approval.

Ocean reflection includes actual scene geometry, with procedural normals and
coastal foam. It is not a fluid simulation. Distance detail culling, compact-mode
decoration and bounded reflection quality are implementation settings, not a
performance acceptance claim.

## Typography and interface

- app/design-system.css owns shared tokens/primitives.
- app/island-game.css owns scoped world HUD, maps and document presentations.
- SiteLayout loads local Inter, Syne and JetBrains Mono.
- Use Syne for headings, Inter for readable body, restrained mono for metadata.
- Keep Projects, Contact, CV and independent reading visible without movement.

The harbor intro explains who Marco is immediately. Starting exploration reduces
the introductory overlay. The map has all destinations from the start; the
optional eight-place visit count is not a game objective or reading requirement.

Nearby objects open HTML immediately with optional camera assistance.
Long text is selectable, semantic web content, never embedded only in a texture.
The old large-board presentation is unmounted.

## Distinct document types

Notebook pages retain a complete opening bio; cases use problem/contribution/
solution/outcome/stack; capability modules connect to actual project examples.
Experience is a two-entry selectable timeline; Education is an archive filtered
by status, with ongoing study unmistakable.

Tesla is an illustrative car/wrap showcase, not a commercial configurator.
Its temporary sample resets on reopening. Dance explicitly separates Athlete
and Teacher without inventing a performance, award, venue or qualification.
Contact uses actual email/social/CV/AI actions; mailto never reports a message
as submitted by this site.

Documents share headings, buttons, focus/close rules and responsive typography,
but not one repeated generic card layout. Use SpotlightSection + Container +
KineticHeading and shared UI controls. Do not restore .surface-card,
.section-copy, .eyebrow, ScrambleText or react-parallax-tilt.

## Content authenticity and accessibility

The concept pictures are architecture references, not project screenshots,
photos of the user's vehicle, or evidence of real dance achievements.
Only confirmed materials can replace explicit missing-content notices.
The confirmed personal references are the 2021 pre-refresh Model 3 and the
Athlete/Teacher dance identities; actual car colour/configuration/modifications,
sports results/media and teaching details are not supplied.

Preserve native disclosure behavior, all eight SSR documents, real links,
keyboard focus, touch cancellation, reduced motion, theme/locale, opt-in audio,
chat persistence and WebGL-failure reading. Controls must not require hovering.
The main world has no scroll-linked animation.

presentations, shared movement surfaces, guided/skippable travel and reader;
generated-reference/Blender/GLB authoring and local review pipeline; seven
static harbor/studio/workshop/archive/laboratory/Dance/Contact aggregates with matching support decks and
simplified hulls; exported rigid-group explorer prototype with verified local
readiness and scoped walking/jump/landing, fade/focus and fallback browser smoke;
five low sloping coasts with shared ocean contact, an unchanged suspended
Skills underside and owned botanical/backdrop layers. There are
twelve static catalog IDs and thirteen editable masters including the separately articulated
explorer; neither static batching nor readiness supplies LOD or final art approval.

Contact continuation: actual revision-2 export, binary/aggregation parity and
experimental seventh building mounting are implemented. Its small hut floor
and 18 solids are mirrored in the shared map. Final lint/build and fresh scoped
CPU gates and final scoped3059 browser smoke pass and are reported separately
in design-qa.md. Projection, contact or browser readiness are not art/FPS approval.

Partial: visual fidelity to the supplied cinematic references, production-grade
character/car assets, new master-asset coverage/integration, full distance
LOD/quality adaptation and secondary factual media. Current visual approval and
measured runtime performance are not implied.

Absent: production-approved asset coverage, skinned/skeletal character, Unity/WebGPU,
authentic dance performance animation and unsupported commercial Tesla/teaching claims.
