## Guided transfers and lifecycle

The engine owns a bounded transfer state, temporarily driving the rendered
explorer while Rapier is stopped. Boat/cannon/parachute props and camera follow
the same position. Completing or skipping restores the supported Rapier arrival.
Reader/chat pauses the transfer; reduced motion finishes it immediately.
There is no boat steering, aiming challenge, unlock, or obligatory travel.

Only the canvas handles movement keys. Blur/pause clears input and inertia;
touch release/cancel/lost capture clears touch input. Hidden documents stop
rendering and pause audio. WebGL failure retains the reader; reload recreates
the renderer, rather than claiming transparent context recovery.

Normal disposal releases owned listeners, RAF, tweens, physics, audio, geometry,
materials, textures and reflection targets, including cached sky environments.
World and terrain constructors roll back their owned allocations if construction
throws; injected-failure and normal-disposal results are recorded in design-qa.
This does not imply every possible GPU/driver/allocation failure is covered.
